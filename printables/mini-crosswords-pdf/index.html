Printable Mini Crosswords PDF for Adults

Mini crosswords are the perfect puzzle size for adults looking for a sharp, rewarding cognitive challenge without the frustrating time commitment of a full Sunday grid. By condensing the format into a tight 5×5 space, these puzzles provide a highly satisfying solving experience that can be completed over a single cup of coffee.

Our crossword construction philosophy focuses on quality and accessibility. You'll find common English words with clever, slightly tricky clues — but absolutely zero obscure trivia or obscure abbreviations. When solving these 5×5 grids, start with the fill-in-the-blank clues or those you are most confident about, then use the crossing letters to unravel the rest. Always remember to match the tense and part of speech of the clue.

This collection includes 3 beautifully symmetrical crossword grids set to an Easy–Medium difficulty. Each grid is a perfect "word square" — an elegant construction where the Across answers match the Down answers, doubling the fun of the interlocking clues.
