Free Printable Pub Quiz Questions & Answers Sheets (PDF)

Welcome to our collection of free pub quiz printable sheets. A great pub quiz requires a balanced mix of subjects, a steady difficulty curve, and clear formatting. Whether you are hosting a trivia night at a local venue, an office party, a family game night, or a school event, these printable question and answer sheets are designed to be ready-to-play instantly.

What Makes a Great Quiz Sheet?

The secret to a memorable quiz is variety. If every question is about world history or sports, players quickly lose interest. Our sheets are carefully curated to cover a mix of history, science, geography, pop culture, and general knowledge. The difficulty is tiered so that everyone can contribute an answer or two, while still offering challenging questions to separate the trivia masters from the casual players.

How to Run Your Trivia Night

As the quizmaster, keeping the game flowing smoothly is essential. We recommend handing out the question sheets directly to the teams, or you can read the questions aloud and have them write their answers down. Scoring is simple: one point per correct answer. If you find yourself in a tie, a quick round of LexiGrid or a rapid-fire memory recall question can serve as an excellent tie-breaker.

Format and Instructions

Each printable PDF round below contains exactly 15 questions. The answer key is printed upside down at the bottom of the sheet. For web users, simply hit the "Print Sheet" button to generate a clean, ink-friendly PDF with no ads or website navigation. Enjoy the game, and may the best team win!
