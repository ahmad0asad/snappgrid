Free daily puzzles for everyone — no account, no app, no nonsense. Open a browser and play.

SnappGrid exists because great puzzle games shouldn't require a subscription, a login, or an app download. We built a place where anyone can play a fresh crossword, word search, sudoku or trivia quiz every single day — for free, from any device.

New puzzles go live every day at midnight. Every past puzzle stays in the archive forever, playable whenever you like. No streaks to maintain, no pressure — just puzzles.

Common questions
Is SnappGrid really free?
Yes, completely. Every game, every day, forever free. We keep the lights on with unobtrusive display advertising.
Do I need to create an account?
No. You can play every game without registering. The optional email subscription is only for daily puzzle reminders.
How often are puzzles updated?
New puzzles go live every day at midnight UTC. Each game type gets a fresh puzzle daily.
Can I play old puzzles?
Yes. Every past puzzle is kept in the archive and remains playable.
Does SnappGrid work on mobile?
Fully. All games are designed mobile-first with on-screen controls and touch support.
How do I unsubscribe from emails?
Every email includes an unsubscribe link. One click removes you immediately.
