About SnappGrid

Puzzles that

Free daily puzzles for everyone — no account, no app, no nonsense. Just open a browser and play.

SnappGrid exists because great puzzle games shouldn't require a subscription, a login, or an app download. We built a place where anyone can play a fresh crossword, word search, sudoku, or trivia quiz every single day — for free, from any device, in seconds.

New puzzles go live every day at midnight. Missed yesterday's? Every past puzzle stays in the archive forever, playable whenever you like. No streaks to maintain, no pressure — just puzzles.

Common questions

FAQ

Is SnappGrid really free?
Yes, completely. Every game, every day, forever free. We keep the lights on with unobtrusive display advertising.
Do I need to create an account?
No. You can play every game without registering. The optional email subscription is only for daily puzzle reminders.
How often are puzzles updated?
New puzzles go live every day at midnight UTC. Each game type (crossword, word search, sudoku, trivia) gets a fresh puzzle daily.
Can I play old puzzles?
Yes. Every past puzzle is kept in the archive and remains playable. Browse the archive from any game page.
Does SnappGrid work on mobile?
Fully. All games are designed mobile-first. The crossword and sudoku have on-screen keypads; word search supports tap-and-drag.
How do I unsubscribe from emails?
Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click removes you immediately.
