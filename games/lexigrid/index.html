📖 How to Play LexiGrid
1
Form Words: Drag/swipe or click adjacent tiles in any direction (horizontal, vertical, diagonal) to spell valid English words (3 to 8 letters).
2
Tile Rules: Tiles cannot be reused within the same word. In classic Boggle fashion, the Qu tile counts as two letters (Q + U).
3
Scoring: 3 letters = 100 pts, 4 letters = 250 pts, 5 letters = 500 pts, 6+ letters = 1,000+ pts.
4
Combo Multiplier: Find words within 5 seconds of each other to boost your score multiplier up to 3.0x!
5
Controls & Accessibility: Use touch, mouse, or arrow keys + Enter to navigate and select tiles on the grid. Type letters directly, press Escape to close modals/clear, or click Rotate to see new angles!
