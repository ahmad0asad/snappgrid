1. Overview

SnappGrid is a free puzzle game site. We aim to collect as little data as possible. You can play every game on this site without creating an account or providing any personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what data SnappGrid ("we", "us", "our") collects when you visit snappgrid.com, how we use it, and what rights you have over it. This policy applies to all visitors from any country, with specific provisions for visitors from the European Union under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data we collect

Data you provide directly:

  • Email address — only if you choose to subscribe to our daily puzzle reminder. This is entirely optional and not required to use any feature of the site.

Data collected automatically:

  • IP address and general location — collected by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) for security and content delivery purposes. We do not store or access this data directly.
  • Browser type and device information — collected via Google Analytics and Google AdSense for analytics and advertising purposes.
  • Pages visited and time spent — aggregated analytics data used to understand how people use the site.
  • Cookies — see the Cookies section below for full details.

We do not collect your name, address, phone number, payment information, or any sensitive personal data. Puzzle progress is stored only in your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to us.

3. How we use your data

  • Email address — used solely to send your daily puzzle reminder email via MailerLite. We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with any third party for their own marketing purposes.
  • Analytics data — used to understand which games are most popular, improve site performance, and fix technical issues.
  • Advertising data — used by Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements. We do not personally target any individual with advertising.

The legal basis for processing your email address is your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). The legal basis for analytics and advertising is our legitimate interest in operating a sustainable free service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Third-party services

SnappGrid uses the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, content delivery, and DDoS protection. Privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google AdSense — advertising. Google may use cookies to serve personalised ads. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics — website analytics. Data is anonymised and aggregated. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • MailerLite — email delivery for puzzle reminders (only if you subscribe). Privacy policy: mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy

Each of these providers has their own privacy policies governing how they handle your data. We encourage you to review them.

We do not sell any personal data to any third party under any circumstances.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. SnappGrid uses the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — set by Google Analytics to track aggregate site usage. You can opt out via your browser settings or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google AdSense to deliver and measure advertisements. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

You can disable non-essential cookies at any time by adjusting your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

EU visitors: by continuing to use this site, you consent to our use of cookies as described above. You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — you may ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — you may ask us to delete your personal data. For email subscribers, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • Right to restrict processing — you may ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — you may request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — you may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting previous processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in the Contact section. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

SnappGrid is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to us without your consent, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Children under 16 in the EU may not subscribe to our email list without parental consent. Our email signup form is intended for adults only.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If we make significant changes that affect how we use your personal data, we will notify email subscribers directly.
