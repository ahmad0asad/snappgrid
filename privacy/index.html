Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Overview

SnappGrid is a free puzzle game site. We aim to collect as little data as possible. You can play every game without creating an account or providing any personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what data SnappGrid collects when you visit snappgrid.com, how we use it, and your rights over it. This policy applies to all visitors, with specific GDPR provisions for EU visitors.

2. Data we collect

Data you provide:

  • Email address — only if you choose to subscribe to our daily puzzle reminder. Entirely optional.

Data collected automatically:

  • IP address and general location — collected by Cloudflare for security and CDN purposes.
  • Browser and device information — via Google Analytics and Google AdSense.
  • Pages visited and time spent — aggregated analytics data.
  • Cookies — see the Cookies section below.

We do not collect your name, address, payment information, or sensitive personal data. Puzzle progress is stored only in your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to us.

3. How we use your data

  • Email address — solely to send daily puzzle reminder emails via MailerLite. Never sold or shared.
  • Analytics data — to improve site performance and understand usage.
  • Advertising data — used by Google AdSense to display advertisements.

4. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare — hosting and CDN. cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google AdSense — advertising. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics — analytics. policies.google.com/privacy
  • MailerLite — email delivery. mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not sell any personal data to any third party under any circumstances.

5. Cookies

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function.
  • Analytics cookies — set by Google Analytics. Opt out via browser settings.
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google AdSense. Manage at adssettings.google.com.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

EU visitors have the right to: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and to object to processing. Email subscribers can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email. Contact us to exercise any rights — we respond within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

SnappGrid is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Children under 16 in the EU may not subscribe without parental consent.
