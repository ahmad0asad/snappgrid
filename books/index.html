5 Sample Questions from Chapter 3: Street Food Legends

Q1. Which country is credited with inventing the croissant?
A) Austria B) France C) Belgium
[Answer: A – Austria]
Q2. TRUE or FALSE: Sushi originally referred to the rice, not the fish.
[Answer: TRUE]
Q3. What spice gives saffron its distinctive golden colour?
[Answer: The stigmas of Crocus sativus flowers]
Q4. Quick Fire: Name the national dish of Japan in 5 seconds.
[Answer: Various — commonly ramen or sushi]
Q5. Fact Bomb: The world's most expensive spice by weight is _____.
[Answer: Saffron]

5 Sample Questions from Chapter 2: Covered Up & Classified

Q1. Which US president's illness was hidden from the public for over a year during wartime?
A) FDR B) JFK C) Woodrow Wilson
[Answer: C – Wilson's stroke in 1919]
Q2. TRUE or FALSE: The Great Fire of London was started deliberately as a government cover-up.
[Answer: FALSE — widely debated but evidence points to accidental]
Q3. What ancient empire's library held an estimated 500,000 scrolls before being destroyed?
[Answer: The Library of Alexandria]
Q4. Quick Fire: Name the CIA's mind-control research program active from the 1950s.
[Answer: MKUltra]
Q5. Fact Bomb: A US senator introduced a bill to ban _____ in 1938, calling it a communist plot.
[Answer: Sliced bread — it was briefly banned in WWII]

5 Unbelievable-But-True Sample Facts

Q1. TRUE or FALSE: Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid.
[Answer: TRUE — she lived ~2,500 yrs after the pyramid, Moon landing was only ~2,000 yrs later]
Q2. What colour is a polar bear's skin under its white fur?
[Answer: Black]
Q3. Quick Fire: How many hearts does an octopus have?
[Answer: Three]
Q4. Fact Bomb: Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire. TRUE or FALSE?
[Answer: TRUE — Oxford ~1096 AD, Aztec Empire ~1300 AD]
Q5. Which planet rains diamonds?
A) Neptune B) Saturn C) Both
[Answer: C — both Neptune & Saturn]
